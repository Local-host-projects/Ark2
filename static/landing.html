Information, not knowledge.
Chaos, then comprehension.

We read feeds, not textbooks. ARK turns history into the thing we already know how to consume — a stream, in the moment, from the people who lived it. Rumor and propaganda bubble to the top, exactly as they did then; your brain does the ordering.

01 · The cast

Who you'll follow

Half the cast are needle-movers — leaders and decision-makers. A third shape the noise into narrative the public can hold: the press and analysts. The last fifth are ordinary people and passersby, who live through it and tell you how it felt.

02 · The clock

One moment at a time

Years compress into a stream of moments, each arriving on its own schedule. Nobody in the feed knows how any of it ends — and neither can you, until you have lived the moments that came before.

03 · The inner life

Emotion & relationships

Every agent carries a live emotional state — fear, resolve, hope — that the day's events bend. Allies, rivals and enemies shape who answers whom, and who stays silent.

04 · The desk

Research layer

Every open moment has a briefing. The narrators organize the chaos; the desk gives you the verified context behind it, when you want more than the feed.
